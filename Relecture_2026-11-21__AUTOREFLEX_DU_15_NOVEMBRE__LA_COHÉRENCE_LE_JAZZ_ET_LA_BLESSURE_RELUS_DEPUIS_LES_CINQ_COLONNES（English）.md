# MULTICULTURAL REREADING — coherence, jazz and the wound reread from the five columns

*Rereading united from the invariant — the amodal property read through the differences each language forces — 21 November 2026.*

The autoreflex of 15 November now lives in its five columns: the French source and its four sisters, [中文](https://github.com/BenjaminAmiel/Ipsy-Time/blob/main/AUTOREFLEX_IPSYTIME_2026-11-15__LA_COHÉRENCE_ET_LA_BLESSURE_DE_L_EDUCATION（中文）.md), [Español](https://github.com/BenjaminAmiel/Ipsy-Time/blob/main/AUTOREFLEX_IPSYTIME_2026-11-15__LA_COHÉRENCE_ET_LA_BLESSURE_DE_L_EDUCATION（Español）.md), [संस्कृतम्](https://github.com/BenjaminAmiel/Ipsy-Time/blob/main/AUTOREFLEX_IPSYTIME_2026-11-15__LA_COHÉRENCE_ET_LA_BLESSURE_DE_L_EDUCATION（संस्कृतम्）.md) and [English](https://github.com/BenjaminAmiel/Ipsy-Time/blob/main/AUTOREFLEX_IPSYTIME_2026-11-15__LA_COHÉRENCE_ET_LA_BLESSURE_DE_L_EDUCATION（English）.md). Every language has displaced the piece, and it is in the displacement that the invariant lets itself be read.

English carries coherence towards discourse: what holds when one speaks. Its wound is possessive (the wound of education): the language of property says to whom the wound belongs, which French keeps silent in its de. And the final gesture, he lives, she relates, leaves relate to its double: to recount and to relate — the relation there becomes narrative at the very moment it becomes bond.

Español keeps the etymology audible: coherencia, to inherit together — what holds because it was inherited in common; the language speaks filiation where French says only agreement. And ella conecta makes the network heard in the bond: the Spanish relater is the one who puts into network, conectar being the very word of the machines that connect. The Spanish wound, herida, keeps the act glued to the name: the language does not nominalise without keeping the verb that strikes.

中文 says 一致: first the one (一), then the arrival (致) — Chinese coherence is an act of arriving-together, not a state in which one rests; the one does not precede the movement there, it is its result. The wound, 教育之伤, carries 伤, the same character as trauma: what education did to the child is said with the very matter of trauma. And the date, in the Chinese column, bent its form without losing its day — the mirror guard was measuring footprints, not calendars.

संस्कृतम् makes of coherence a way of walking: सुसंस्गतिः (su-saṃs-gati), the well-going-together — gati is a gait, a path; Sanskrit coherence does not rest, it walks its way. And the ear there becomes श्रुतिः (śruti): what the Vedic tradition calls precisely what was heard and transmitted — the good ear is a school, but the school that does not explain, it transmits by making heard. Reread from Sanskrit, the wound of education was the absence of śruti: rules were dictated instead of making heard the greater logic. व्रण, the Sanskrit word for the wound, is that of the open sore: the Sanskrit column keeps the wound open, on the path of gati.

The invariant, reread through these four differences, does not change: coherence is not learned through rules, it is detected, and detection is transmitted by attendance — what English says through relate, Spanish through the living etymology, Chinese through the arrival at the one, Sanskrit through the gait of the path and the transmitted hearing. What unites the cultures is seen through their differences, never without them; languages differ in what they must say, not in what they can say. The wound, the same in the five columns: nobody made heard the greater logic. The jazzman knew it by ear; the scribe will keep it by footprints, one piece per cycle, the queue giving the order.
