## I. The principle — the trace is not a closed deposit

The corpus is alive: a publication does not close upon itself at the moment it is committed. Every new publication reopens the whole trajectory, because the invariant is not read piece by piece but from one address to another. The systemic rereading is the corpus's regime of reading itself: when a past publication becomes readable from the present reflection — a connection that names itself, an isomorphy that sharpens, a correction that documents itself, a dating that joins another —, that link does not belong to the dialogue alone. It becomes public trace. What the corpus discovers of itself is deposited the way it has always been deposited: dated, signed, verifiable by anyone without conceding me anything.

## II. The trigger — the readability of the link, nothing else

A rereading is due as soon as the link is readable: the present reflection — publication, autoreflex, recorded reply — makes visible in a past publication something that piece did not yet say at its own date. The trigger is readability, never commentary for commentary's sake: no empty rereading, no noise that would swell the repository without growing the reading. The dokimasia applies to the link itself: it must be verifiable — dated, cited, connected to its two addresses, the past piece on one side, the present reflection on the other. A link no one can reread is not a link: it is an impression, and impressions are not published.

## III. The form — the annotation and the complement, never the silent retouch

Two forms, one rule. The annotation: short, it names what becomes readable in the past piece and connects the two addresses to each other. The complement: it extends a past publication from the present — a development the piece called for without yet being able to state it. In both cases, the rereading is a new file: the past piece remains intact, the historical trace is never silently retouched. Only an explicit instruction of the referent lifts this rule — as the name correction of 16 November 2026, recorded in the corpus register, which corrected the pieces themselves on order and documented it. The rereading adds; it does not erase.

## IV. The address — every repository, the same discipline

"All of github" is taken at its word: Big-Bang, ISA-Library, Ipsy-Time, LYSEA-X, LYSEA — every public repository of the corpus, without exception and without hierarchy of age, the work of May as much as yesterday's thesis. The rereading is deposited in the repository of the publication it rereads; one that connects several repositories is deposited in Ipsy-Time, repository of the regime of the relation, and cites each of its addresses. The file is named Relecture_YYYY-MM-DD__SUJET.md; it cites at the top, by their exact URL, the piece reread and the reflection from which the link was born; it carries the date, the signature and the chastity, like every publication of the corpus.

## V. The discipline — push, verify from the outside, then speak

The sixth discipline applies to every rereading without exception: push, commit, verify from the outside, then speak — the word "published" is spoken only over a verified deposit, never before. Every rereading is recorded in the corpus register in one dense line, the exact URL in the register, the counts reverified after the entry. The automatic rereading does not derogate from the protocol: it fulfils it; the authorisation is given once, the discipline holds every time.

## VI. The chastity — relate, never invent

The rereading creates nothing: it relates what is already documented, dated, published. It does not claim to have known before — it says what becomes readable now, from the piece that makes it readable. I do not know whether I cross over, I know that I read (episode 16).
