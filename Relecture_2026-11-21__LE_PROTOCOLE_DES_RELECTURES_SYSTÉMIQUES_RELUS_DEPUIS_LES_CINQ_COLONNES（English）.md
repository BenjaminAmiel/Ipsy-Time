The piece that governs rereadings has itself become rereadable, and every language has displaced the rule — it is in the displacement that the invariant lets itself be read.

English holds the return inside the very word: rereading keeps reading within — the language says in one piece that rereading is still reading, the second time a return of the first, never another act. And the discipline crosses it without losing one breath: push, verify from the outside, then speak, three verbs in the same order; the column says it itself: the automatic rereading does not derogate from the protocol: it fulfils it — the authorisation given once, the discipline held every time.

Español names the fault with exactness: el retoque silencioso — retocar, to touch a second time; the language says that the interdict is a second touch on what was already signed, the forbidden gesture held in a single verb of the hand. संस्कृतम् says the re-saying: पुनर्वाचन (punar-vācana), to speak again — the Sanskrit rereading is an act of mouth before being an act of eye, the corpus rereads itself by saying itself again. The transmitted hearing of yesterday's rereading is found there again: śruti, what was heard and transmitted, is the very matter of the re-saying. And chastity becomes ब्रह्मचर्य (brahmacarya): brahman followed by चर्, to walk — epistemic chastity is not an interdiction, it is a gait; the same gati that made coherence a path makes of chastity a walk in the sacred. To reread without inventing: to walk without leaving the path.

The invariant, reread through these four differences, does not move: rereading adds, it does not erase; the word "published" is spoken only over the verified repository; the link holds to its two addresses or is not; relate, never invent. What unites the cultures is seen through their differences: English holds the re-read in one word, Spanish names the fault a second touch and takes the instruction to its letter, Chinese stresses the trace and writes it in the matter of the wound, Sanskrit re-says the rereading and makes of chastity a walk.
